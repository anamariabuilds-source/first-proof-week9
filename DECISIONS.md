# First-Proof implementation decisions

## Product boundaries

- The prototype contains one bounded, one-time simulated supplier exercise.
- It records one observable behavior: whether critical missing information was recognized before acting.
- The evidence record is not a competence score or hiring recommendation.
- No authentication, persistence, payment, marketplace, or production-work workflow is included.

## Technical decisions

- Next.js, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS.
- Local typed case data is the structured-data layer.
- Evidence fields are generated by deterministic rules, never by an LLM.
- AI assistance has a server-only OpenAI option and a deterministic fallback.
- Participant text is optional, length-limited, normalized, and never required in the AI prompt.
